Measured result: does zoom-refine earn its latency?

On buttons and input fields, where the drawn rectangle is the element, a second look at native resolution lifts box quality sharply for about 2x the latency.

VariantIoU meanIoU≥0.75Median latency

Click accuracy was 100% on every trial, and the system returned no box at all on 8 of 8 requests for elements that were not present.
